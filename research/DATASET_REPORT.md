## Reproducibility and limitations

- Original source metadata snapshots: research/*-metadata.json. Local audit: research/local_dataset_audit.json. Download audit: research/candidate_audit.json.
- Candidate CSVs are held in an ignored research/candidate-downloads directory for review, not included in model training or publication.
- Cross-source compatibility must establish shared diagnosis criteria, patient identity, units, measurement procedures, collection sites and licenses. Missing feature values must not be invented to force a merge.
- Outliers were audited using 1.5×IQR for selected numeric features and retained. Elevated lab values may be clinically meaningful; deletion without clinical justification can bias sensitivity.
- The legacy dataset's “Cycle length(days)” has values consistent with short bleeding duration. Its clinical codebook meaning is unverified. It is not automatically populated from period start-to-start interval in the website.
- SMOTE appears only inside the training pipeline and folds. Its synthetic training points are not represented as additional real patients. The current numeric-encoded categorical predictors can be interpolated by standard SMOTE; this remains an experimental limitation. A separately validated SMOTENC / categorical encoding study is appropriate future work, not a reason to relabel this run as clinically validated.
